# Laptop upgrade thoughts

Wrist has been bothering me after long Budget CLI sessions on the kitchen table — probably the laptop sitting too low, not the laptop itself. Before spending money on a new machine, try a stand + external keyboard for two weeks and see if it's actually a hardware problem or just posture.

If it's still bad after that: current laptop is fine for everything I actually do (terminal, browser, a PDF reader for course books) — no reason to upgrade the machine itself, just the desk setup.
